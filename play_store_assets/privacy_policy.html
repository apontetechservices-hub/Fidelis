Fidelis — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

No Data Collection

Fidelis is a traditional Catholic prayer companion app. We respect your privacy and keep your data where it belongs — on your device.

We collect no personal data. Fidelis does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information whatsoever.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to any server:

  • Prayer preferences and settings
  • Notification schedules you configure
  • Rosary progress and bookmark state

You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings.

Internet Usage

Fidelis uses the internet for one purpose only:

  • Daily Mass Readings — fetched from publicly available liturgical sources (Divinum Officium for 1962 Traditional Missal, USCCB for Novus Ordo readings)

No personal data is sent in these requests. The app functions offline for all other features (Rosary, Chaplet, Stations of the Cross, common prayers, etc.).

Third-Party Services

Fidelis does not use:

  • Analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
  • Advertising networks or ad SDKs
  • Crash reporting services (Crashlytics, Sentry, etc.)
  • Social media SDKs
  • Any third-party tracking of any kind

Notifications

Fidelis sends local notifications only (e.g., daily prayer reminders). These are scheduled on your device and require your permission. No data about your notifications is collected or transmitted.

Children's Privacy

Fidelis is suitable for all ages. Since we collect no personal data, there are no special concerns regarding children.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change what data Fidelis collects (which we have no plans to do), we will update this policy and the app will require you to accept the changes.
